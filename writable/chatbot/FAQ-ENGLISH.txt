=== KNOWLEDGE BASE: PT PELINDO MARINE SERVICE (ENGLISH) ===

## A. COMPANY PROFILE

Q: What is PT Pelindo Marine Service?
A: PT Pelindo Marine Service (Pelindo Marine) is a subsidiary of PT Pelindo Jasa Maritim, part of the Pelindo Group, providing integrated maritime services including tugboat and pilot boat operations, ship agency services, ship repair and maintenance facilities, dredging and reclamation, energy logistics and shorebase services, and FAME transportation.

Q: What is Pelindo Marine's vision?
A: To become a reliable integrated maritime solutions provider with an extensive network across Indonesian and international waters.

Q: Where is Pelindo Marine's head office located?
A: Pelindo Place, 16th Floor, Jalan Perak Timur No. 478, Surabaya 60165, Indonesia.

Q: When was Pelindo Marine established?
A: Pelindo Marine was established on December 31, 2011, evolving from the shipping unit of Pelindo III into an independent company.

Q: Is Pelindo Marine part of the state-owned enterprise Pelindo?
A: Yes. Pelindo Marine operates under PT Pelindo Jasa Maritim, which is part of PT Pelabuhan Indonesia (Persero), a state-owned enterprise.

Q: What are Pelindo Marine's main services?
A: Ship pilotage, towage, ship agency, shipyard and ship repair, dredging and reclamation, energy logistics and shorebase, FAME transportation, port utilities, and integrated logistics.

Q: In which areas does Pelindo Marine operate?
A: Pelindo Marine serves various ports across Indonesia, from Aceh to Papua, covering more than 56 strategic operational locations.

Q: What impact did the 2021 Pelindo integration have on Pelindo Marine?
A: Following the Pelindo integration on October 1, 2021, Pelindo Marine came under PT Pelindo Jasa Maritim as Pelindo's maritime services sub-holding.

Q: Does Pelindo Marine have subsidiaries?
A: Yes, including PT Alur Pelayaran Barat Surabaya (APBS) and PT Pelindo Energi Logistik (PEL).

Q: Who are Pelindo Marine's main customers?
A: Ship owners, shipping agents, terminal operators, energy companies, logistics companies, and businesses requiring maritime services.

Q: Can Pelindo Marine's services be customized?
A: Yes. Services can be tailored to customer operational needs while complying with regulations and fleet availability.

Q: How can I contact Pelindo Marine?
A: Customers can reach Pelindo Marine through the official website, company contacts, or the e-PPID service.

Q: What is Pelindo Marine's commitment to safety?
A: Safety is a top priority through the implementation of occupational health and safety (K3) culture, operational SOPs, and continuous improvement of workforce competency.

Q: Does Pelindo Marine engage in long-term partnerships?
A: Yes. Partnerships can be established through service contracts, MoUs, or other collaboration schemes.

Q: What are Pelindo Marine's key strengths?
A: Being part of the Pelindo Group, a nationwide operational network, a fleet distributed across Indonesia, international safety standards, and integrated maritime solutions.

Q: What is the company's commitment to digitalization?
A: Pelindo Marine continues to develop fleet monitoring systems, digital-based customer services, and digitalization of operational processes.

## B. PILOTAGE & TOWAGE

Q: What is ship pilotage?
A: A navigation service performed by licensed pilots to ensure safe vessel maneuvering when entering, leaving, or moving within port areas.

Q: What is ship towage?
A: A tugboat assistance service that helps vessels during berthing, unberthing, anchoring, and other maritime operations.

Q: Does Pelindo Marine provide pilot boats and tugboats?
A: Yes, Pelindo Marine operates a fleet of pilot boats and tugboats for various maritime operational needs.

Q: Can tugboats be used for barge operations?
A: Yes. Pelindo Marine provides tugboat services for barge operations and other project activities.

Q: Are offshore services available?
A: Yes, services can be provided for offshore operations according to needs and applicable regulations.

Q: How do I book pilotage or towage services?
A: Customers can submit a request through official channels, including vessel data, operation schedule, and service requirements, to the Commercial Team at +62 811 3330 4181.

Q: Are services available 24 hours a day?
A: Operational services are adjusted according to needs and port schedules as well as relevant authorities.

Q: What safety standards apply during vessel maneuvering?
A: All operations are carried out in accordance with SOPs, safety standards, and applicable occupational health and safety (K3) regulations.

Q: Does Pelindo Marine have experience in strategic shipping lanes?
A: Yes. Pelindo Marine has supported operations in strategic areas such as the Malacca Strait, Phillips Strait, and Singapore Strait.

Q: How is the cost of pilotage and towage services determined?
A: Costs are determined based on vessel type, vessel size, service location, maneuver complexity, and service duration.

Q: What is Bollard Pull?
A: Bollard Pull is the maximum towing capacity of a tugboat, used as an indicator of a tugboat's strength.

Q: How many tugboats are needed for one vessel?
A: The number of tugboats depends on vessel size, tonnage, port conditions, and applicable safety regulations.

## C. SHIPYARD & SHIP REPAIR

Q: What shipyard services are available?
A: Pelindo Marine provides ship maintenance and repair facilities, including both dry docking and afloat repair.

Q: Is a graving dock facility available?
A: Yes. Pelindo Marine has a graving dock facility for docking and vessel maintenance work.

Q: Can emergency repairs be handled?
A: Yes, depending on the availability of facilities, materials, technical personnel, and the urgency of the work.

Q: How do I request a shipyard quotation?
A: Customers can submit work specifications, docking plans, technical drawings, and target completion time.

Q: Is pre-docking inspection available?
A: Yes. A technical team can conduct an initial inspection to determine the scope of work.

Q: Can Pelindo Marine supply spare parts?
A: Yes, subject to project needs and the availability of approved suppliers.

Q: What quality standards are applied to the work?
A: All work follows vessel classification standards and applicable quality procedures.

Q: Does shipyard work implement occupational health and safety (K3) standards?
A: Yes. All shipyard activities must comply with workplace safety and environmental standards.

Q: Does Pelindo Marine serve privately owned vessels?
A: Yes. Shipyard facilities serve government-owned, state-owned enterprise, and national or international private vessels.

Q: What is afloat repair?
A: Ship repair performed without dry docking, so the vessel remains in the water throughout the process.

## D. DREDGING & RECLAMATION

Q: What dredging services are available?

Q: Does Pelindo Marine have dredging project experience?
A: Yes. Pelindo Marine has handled various dredging projects across Indonesia.

Q: How is the dredging method determined?
A: The method is determined based on hydro-oceanographic survey results, sediment type, and target depth.

Q: Do dredging projects require environmental permits?
A: Yes. All projects must comply with applicable environmental and permitting regulations.

Q: How long does a dredging project take?

Q: How is dredged material managed?

A: Shore-based positions can be filled by candidates from various educational backgrounds. However, seafaring positions generally require maritime education and certification.

Q: What is the difference between Pelindo and Pelindo Marine?
A: Pelindo focuses on port management, while Pelindo Marine focuses on ship services and maritime operations.

Q: Does Pelindo Marine operate at sea or at the port?
A: Both. Operations cover port areas as well as surrounding waters.

Q: Are all vessels required to use pilotage services?
A: Not all. The requirement depends on vessel size and local port regulations.

Q: Why are tugboats small in size but very powerful?
A: Because they are designed with high-power engines and specialized propulsion systems to tow or push other vessels.

Q: What is the difference between a tugboat and a merchant vessel?
A: A tugboat is used to assist the maneuvering of other vessels, while a merchant vessel is used to transport goods or passengers.

Q: How long does the berthing process take?
